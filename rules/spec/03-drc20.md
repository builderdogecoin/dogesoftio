# 03. DRC-20 Coins

DRC-20 Coins are plain-text JSON inscriptions with `"p":"drc-20"` and an `op` of `deploy`, `mint` or `transfer`. Doge Soft follows the DRC-20 protocol rules strictly. Below are the 15 protocol rules exactly as the index checks them against Dogecoin Core, in order.

1. **Exactly 4 bytes.** `tick` is exactly 4 bytes of UTF-8, measured on the raw string as inscribed. A tick with a NUL byte is skipped.
2. **Case doesn't matter.** Ticks are case-insensitive and stored lowercase (plain Unicode lowercase, letter by letter), after rule 1. `DOGI`, `Dogi` and `dogi` are one Coin.
3. **Look-alikes are never merged.** `𝕏`, `𝑿`, `ｘ` and `x` are different ticks, and so are `d0ge` and `doge`.
4. **No padding.** A tick with spaces around it is rejected, not trimmed: `" abc"` is not `"abc"`.
5. **Strict numbers.** `max`, `lim`, `amt` and `dec` are JSON strings. Amounts are ASCII digits with an optional `.` and digits on both sides (`1.`, `.5`, `1e8`, `+1` and spaces are rejected; `007` is fine). At most 18 decimals, never more than the Coin's `dec`, above zero, and no more than 18,446,744,073,709,551,615. `dec` is 18 when absent, otherwise a string from `"0"` to `"18"`.
6. **First deploy wins.** The first valid deploy of a tick, in chain order, creates the Coin.
7. **Invalid deploys are skipped.** A broken deploy doesn't block the tick; the next valid deploy can still win.
8. **Deploy before mint.** A mint only counts after a valid deploy, in chain order.
9. **Respect the limit.** A mint with `amt` above `lim` gets nothing.
10. **Max supply is max supply.** The mint that crosses `max` gets only the remainder. Once `max` is reached, mints get nothing.
11. **Transfers spent as a fee.** A transfer inscription spent as a fee when it is inscribed is ignored. One spent as a fee when it is sent goes back to the sender. Details [below](#rule-11).
12. **Inscribe, then send.** A transfer counts only when it is inscribed with enough available balance, then sent. Each transfer inscription works once; later moves change nothing.
13. **Balances come from the chain.** Only valid mints and sent transfers change balances, in chain order.
14. **Holders.** An address holds a Coin while its available plus transferable balance is above zero.
15. **`doge` is not a DRC-20 Coin.** The exact tick `doge` (any case) is never listed. Its look-alikes are their own ticks.

## Rule 11: transfers spent as a fee {#rule-11}

A transfer is two steps: inscribe, then send. If either step spends the transfer inscription as a fee, here's what happens:

- **Spent as a fee when inscribed** (its first koinu lands in no output): the inscription is ignored. It is not a valid transfer and no balance changes.
- **Spent as a fee when sent** (the second step): the amount goes back to the sender's available balance. Nobody else gets it, and nothing is lost.

Sending a transfer inscription into an `OP_RETURN` output is different: that burns the amount (see [04. Burns](04-burns.md)).

## Holders {#holders}

An address is a holder of a coin while its available balance plus its open transfer locks is above zero. The holder count changes only when an address's total crosses zero.
